# Research notes: disentangling MOSA

Living record for the MSc project. Sections: what the question is, what was
built, what was tested and the outcome, what did not work, technical
limitations, promising directions, and applications. Literature is in
`LITERATURE.md`. Numbers below come from `results/tables/metrics_summary.csv`
and are regenerated by the pipeline; dates mark when each entry was written.

## 1. Question

MOSA's latent space is entangled: technical variation (laboratory, platform,
model system) is superimposed on biology (tissue, EMT, ...). The thesis asks
whether the main factors can be separated into dedicated latent blocks
without losing reconstruction performance, and whether the resulting model
gives (i) batch-removed reconstructions and (ii) interpretable blocks that stay
informative for drug response and gene essentiality.

## 2. What was built (2026-11-08)

- `DisentangledVAE`, a subclass of the released MOSA VAE with a latent layout
  `[model_type | tissue | emt | free]`. Block widths and factor columns are
  declared in the dataset config; variants switch regularisers on and off.
- Three families of regulariser, each independently weightable per factor:
  supervised heads (block predicts its factor), leakage discriminators
  (factor predicted from the *other* blocks is penalised; `flip` or
  `confusion` mode), and normalised-HSIC (CKA) penalties between the other
  blocks and the factor, plus pairwise CKA between blocks. DIP-VAE-II is
  available for the unsupervised comparison.
- `decoder_conditionals` controls which parts of MOSA's conditional vector
  the decoders see, so the source can be removed from the decoder and forced
  through the source block (MIDAS design).
- Counterfactual decoding: swap the source conditional and/or replace a block
  with a prototype vector (`DisentangledVAE.predict`).
- Evaluation per fold: held-out NMSE per view and cross-view imputation;
  block-by-factor probe matrix with chance-corrected own/leak summaries;
  kNN source mixing and tissue silhouette in the biological blocks; ridge R2
  to drug response and CRISPR; NMSE of counterfactual reconstructions against
  the batch-free truth (observed entries and views missing by design).
- Data: the public MOSA cell line tables, an EMT score, a simulated
  three-level technical factor with ground truth at three confounding levels,
  and a real two-laboratory proteomics dataset.

(Results sections are filled in below once the benchmark has run.)

## 3. Engineering issues met while building the basis (2026-11-08)

- `mosalib` pins `torch==2.14.0`; the default wheel targets CUDA 13 and fails
  on the group's driver (550, CUDA 12.4). Fixed by installing
  `torch==2.14.0+cu126`. Keep this in the environment instructions.
- `MOSADataModule._get_weighted_sampler` only accepts MOSA's own dataset
  classes, so wrapping the dataset to add factor targets needed an override
  (`FactorDataModule._get_weighted_sampler`). Upstreaming the factor targets
  into `MOSADataset` removes the wrapper altogether.
- The public figshare deposit has no organoids or tumours with omics; the
  `proteomics_ccle.csv` columns are mostly Sanger identifiers, which gives 290
  cell lines measured in two laboratories. That paired design was not
  mentioned in the paper and is the only real technical factor available
  publicly.
- MOSA's reconstruction NMSE normalises by the observed variance of a view.
  For the counterfactual scores the normaliser is the variance of the
  ground-truth (reference-domain) values, so scores are comparable across
  decoding strategies but not with the ordinary NMSE of the same view.
- Early stopping on `val/loss` with patience 25 stops most runs after 45 to 60
  epochs at the default learning rate; the comparison is therefore at equal
  stopping rule, not at equal epochs.
- The normalised HSIC (CKA) penalty diverged to NaN at a source weight of 50
  (two of three folds in every `sweep_*_hsic50` variant of pass 2) and the
  logistic probe then crashed the fold. Two causes: the median-heuristic
  bandwidth was differentiated through, so the encoder could shrink a block
  instead of decorrelating it, and the CKA denominator went to zero once a
  block collapsed. Fixed by detaching the bandwidth and adding an epsilon to
  the denominator (`losses.gaussian_kernel`, `losses.cka`). `evaluate_run`
  now records `diverged = 1` instead of failing, so a sweep that hits an
  unstable setting still completes and the failure is visible in the tables.
  MOSA's `gradient_clip_val` defaults to 0 (no clipping); turning it on is
  the obvious next safeguard when exploring strong penalties.

## 4. Technical limitations of the current basis

- The simulated technical factor is linear (feature shifts, rescaling, a
  coherent programme) plus view loss. Real model-type effects (culture
  selection, stromal contamination in tumours, organoid media) are
  non-linear and biology-dependent; conclusions about *how much* correction
  is possible transfer only qualitatively.
- Factor labels are needed for every training sample; samples with unknown
  tissue or EMT are masked out of the factor losses but still trained on.
  Missing-label robustness has not been evaluated.
- EMT is computed from the transcriptomics view, so the EMT head is partly
  self-supervised: the block can predict the score from the same genes that
  define it. Use the proteomics-based EMT score (`emt_proteomics`) or an
  external label to test generalisation.
- Linear probes with 60+ latent dimensions and strong batch effects are very
  sensitive: a factor can be "leaked" at probe accuracy 0.9 while kNN mixing
  already looks acceptable. Report both and, for strong claims, a non-linear
  probe as well.
- The adversarial `flip` mode (maximising discriminator loss, MOSA's choice)
  is unbounded and can destabilise training at large weights; `confusion`
  mode is bounded but weaker. Neither was tuned beyond the sweep in §6.
- CKA penalties are computed per minibatch (64 samples): noisy for
  categorical factors with many classes (tissue, 22 levels). Larger batches
  or a running estimate would help.
- One seed per fold; fold-to-fold spread is reported but seed variance is not.
- Checkpoints are only kept for the showcase runs (`save_models` in the
  pipeline config) to limit disk use.

## 5. Promising directions for the thesis

1. **MIDAS-style hard separation.** Remove the source from the decoder
   conditionals (`supervised_nocond`) and let the source block carry it, with
   a stronger leakage penalty on the biological blocks. This is the cleanest
   route to a latent space whose biological part is source-free by
   construction, and it makes block replacement the single counterfactual
   mechanism.
2. **Conditional, not marginal, independence.** Pass 2 (section 8) shows
   that pushing the source block to be independent of the rest with a
   marginal CKA penalty is monotonically harmful: it removes tissue and EMT
   information (which are correlated with the source under confounding)
   without lowering linear leakage. The penalty worth building is
   independence *given tissue*: HSIC or MMD computed within tissue strata
   (Lopez et al. 2018 condition on the batch in the same spirit), or a
   leakage discriminator that also receives the tissue label so that it can
   only exploit source information that tissue does not explain.
3. **A better leakage measure.** A linear probe at balanced accuracy 0.9-1.0
   can rest on a single latent direction. Report leakage as a curve
   (accuracy after projecting out the top-k source directions), as the
   mutual information estimated by a non-linear estimator, or as the change
   in downstream predictions when the source block is replaced. Only then is
   it possible to say whether 0.9 is a problem.
4. **Prototype replacement vs conditional swap.** With a source block,
   counterfactual decoding can swap the block for the reference prototype.
   Compare with sampling the block from the reference distribution and with
   per-tissue prototypes, which keep tissue-specific technical effects.
5. **Interpretable free block.** Tie free-block dimensions to gene programmes
   with a masked linear decoder (expiMap/VEGA) on the transcriptomics view, or
   fit a linear decoder on top of the trained latent to read loadings.
6. **Real model-type factor.** Run the same pipeline on the internal cell line
   + organoid + tumour cohort. Celligner-style tumour/cell line pairs (same
   cancer type) give a sanity check similar to the paired proteomics here.
7. **Semi-supervised factors.** Many tumours lack EMT scores; MIDAS and scANVI
   show that a classifier head on a subset of labelled samples suffices.
   The masks in `FactorDataModule` already support this.

## 6. Applications worth developing

- **Batch-removed omics for release.** Decode every sample as the reference
  model type: a cell-line-domain version of tumour and organoid profiles that
  can be fed to models trained on cell lines (drug response, essentiality).
- **Cross-laboratory harmonisation.** The paired proteomics shows the
  mechanism on a real two-laboratory effect; the same applies to GDSC1/GDSC2
  drug screens or Sanger/Broad CRISPR screens inside MOSA.
- **Counterfactual queries.** Move a sample along the EMT block and decode to
  predict which drugs or dependencies change with EMT state; swap tissue
  blocks to ask "what would this line look like if it came from tissue X".
- **Interpretation.** Blocks give a direct reading of what the model uses:
  source block norm as a technical-effect score per sample, tissue block
  distances as a data-driven tissue similarity, EMT block as a continuous axis
  that can be correlated with proteomic/phospho data.

## 7. Results, pass 1 (2026-11-08; tables in `results/tables/pass1/`)

Eight variants, four datasets, three folds, early stopping (patience 25,
most runs stop at 45-70 epochs), one seed. EMT in this pass still included
haematopoietic lines (fixed in pass 2). Means over folds; NMSE lower is
better, probes higher is better except `*_leak`.

### Simulated sources, moderate confounding (`sim_c05`)

| variant | NMSE (7 views) | counterfactual NMSE: none / cond / cond+block | source leak (bio blocks) | tissue own / leak | source mixing (bio) | drug R2 (bio) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| baseline | 0.427 | 0.71 / 0.43 / - | - | - | 0.87 | 0.48 |
| adversarial (flip) | 0.435 | 0.71 / 0.43 / - | - | - | 0.87 | 0.47 |
| dip | 0.527 | 0.82 / 0.51 / - | - | - | 0.57 | 0.25 |
| supervised | 0.431 | 0.76 / 0.49 / 0.43 | 0.82 | 0.60 / 0.42 | 0.79 | 0.48 |
| supervised_nocond | **0.422** | 0.79 / 0.79 / **0.42** | 0.97 | 0.57 / 0.43 | 0.85 | **0.50** |
| hsic | 0.488 | 0.85 / 0.54 / 0.49 | 0.95 | 0.54 / 0.44 | 0.90 | 0.40 |
| midas | 0.435 | 0.77 / 0.50 / 0.44 | 0.95 | 0.58 / 0.49 | 0.84 | 0.49 |
| full | 0.479 | 0.82 / 0.51 / 0.48 | 0.91 | 0.53 / 0.42 | 0.92 | 0.42 |

What worked

- **Supervised blocks cost nothing.** `supervised`, `supervised_nocond` and
  `midas` reconstruct as well as the baseline (NMSE 0.42-0.44 vs 0.43), on
  every view, and keep or improve downstream drug-response R2. The tissue
  block has visible tissue structure (silhouette 0.08-0.14 in the biological
  blocks vs -0.09 for the baseline).
- **Block replacement is a working counterfactual mechanism.** With the
  decoder blind to the source (`supervised_nocond`), swapping the source
  conditional does nothing (as it must), and replacing the source block with
  the reference prototype brings the batch-free NMSE from 0.79 to 0.42, as
  good as MOSA's own conditional swap (0.43) with no reconstruction penalty.
  With both mechanisms present (`supervised`, `midas`), the conditional swap
  alone is *worse* than in the baseline (0.49-0.50): the source block keeps
  feeding the decoder, so both must be swapped together (0.43-0.44).
- **Views missing by design are imputed in the reference domain** at the
  same quality as observed ones (`cf_*_missing` ≈ `cf_*` in
  `metrics_long.csv`), i.e. the model generates the cell-line-domain
  proteomics of a "tumour" that never had proteomics.

What did not work (yet)

- **Linear leakage is not removed.** The source stays linearly predictable
  from the biological blocks at balanced accuracy 0.82-0.97 (chance 0.33) in
  every block variant. Adversarial weight 0.1 and CKA weight 5 are too weak
  against a technical effect that spans ~20,000 features; the sweep in pass 2
  measures what strength is needed and what it costs. kNN mixing looks fine
  (0.8-0.9) at the same time, so mixing alone would have hidden this.
- **CKA penalties as weighted cost reconstruction** (`hsic`, `full`: +0.05
  NMSE, -0.07 drug R2) without fixing leakage; the gradient of a per-batch
  CKA with 22 tissue classes is noisy.
- **DIP-VAE** (the earlier PhenPred direction) is strictly worse: +0.10 NMSE,
  half the downstream R2, no organisation benefit. Not worth pursuing.
- **MOSA's flipped adversary is unstable** on the real two-laboratory data:
  on `proteomics_paired` it *reduced* source mixing from 0.63 (baseline) to
  0.06 and paired-line retrieval from 0.07 to 0.02 (the encoder "wins" by
  making the source trivially separable in a way the lagging discriminator
  mis-predicts). The bounded confusion objective is added in pass 2.
- **EMT leaked badly** (0.75-0.80) because the score was tissue-driven
  (haematopoietic lines are maximally "mesenchymal" by construction); fixed
  in pass 2 by leaving EMT undefined for blood cancers.

### Strong confounding (`sim_c09`)

When tissue almost determines the source, every block variant loses source
mixing in the biological blocks (0.25-0.55 vs 0.83 for the baseline) while
keeping tissue structure: mixing and tissue conservation are in direct
conflict, exactly the trade-off the proposal anticipates. The counterfactual
scores degrade only slightly (0.44-0.50 vs 0.45 for the baseline), so the
decoder still removes the source even when the latent cannot be made
source-free. This dataset is the one to use when arguing about what
"disentangled" should mean under confounding.

The CCLE and Sanger intensities are on different scales, hence the huge

three times the baseline. This is a real, label-free check that the blocks
hold line identity rather than laboratory identity.

### What the second pass established

- **Pass-1 conclusions hold with clean EMT.** Supervised blocks
  (`supervised`, `supervised_nocond`, `midas`) still cost no reconstruction
  on any view (held-out NMSE and cross-view imputation within 0.01 of the
  baseline), keep or improve downstream drug-response and CRISPR R2, give
  the latent space tissue structure the baseline lacks, and triple
  paired-line retrieval on the real two-laboratory data. Block replacement
  in `supervised_nocond` is the best counterfactual on every simulated
  dataset (0.42 / 0.42 / 0.45 at confounding 0 / 0.5 / 0.9, vs 0.43 / 0.43 /
  0.45 for MOSA's conditional swap). Notebook 04 shows the mechanism: the
  residual per-feature shift after decoding collapses from the injected
  shift to near zero only when the source block is replaced.
- **The EMT block reads the EMT programme.** With haematopoietic lines
  excluded, the two EMT coordinates of `supervised_nocond` correlate at
  |r| 0.7-0.8 with VIM, ZEB1/2, SPARC, PDGFRB on one side and RAB25, ST14,
  GRHL2, ESRP1, EPCAM, CDH1 on the other (notebook 03), and the held-out
  probe R2 of the block is 0.88. EMT remains predictable from the other
  blocks (R2 0.79): a continuous score computed from the transcriptome is a
  smooth function of the cell state that the free block must encode to
  reconstruct expression, so "no EMT outside the EMT block" is not an
  attainable or even desirable target. The useful statement is that the
  block isolates the programme into two interpretable coordinates.
- **Marginal independence penalties are counterproductive.** The sweep is
  monotone in the wrong direction: raising the source CKA weight from 5 to
  50 raises NMSE from 0.50 to 0.55, raises linear leakage from 0.89 to
  0.94-0.97, and destroys the tissue block (own-factor probe 0.56 -> 0.18)
  and, at 50, the EMT block. The adversarial weight (0.1 to 5, confusion
  mode) changes nothing. Under confounding, source and tissue share
  information; forcing the source block to be independent of the rest
  removes the tissue signal from the biological blocks rather than the
  source signal. After the numerical fix the CKA variants also cost more
  than in pass 1 (+0.07 NMSE instead of +0.05), because the encoder can no
  longer satisfy the penalty by rescaling a block. This is the clearest
  negative result of the basis and motivates direction 2 of section 5.
- **Linear leakage is not fixed by any variant tried.** The source remains
  linearly decodable from the biological blocks at balanced accuracy
  0.85-0.98 (chance 0.33) in simulation and 1.0 on the paired proteomics,
  while kNN mixing of the same blocks is 0.8-0.9. A probe that reaches 1.0
  on 4000 proteins measured in two laboratories is almost certainly reading
  a single scale or missingness direction, which is why a leakage *curve*
  (direction 3 of section 5) is needed before leakage can be optimised.
- **Adversaries hurt on the real data.** Both the flipped and the confusion
  objective collapse source mixing on `proteomics_paired` (0.63 -> 0.06 and
  0.01) and halve paired retrieval; on the simulations they are neutral. A
  discriminator on a single view with two perfectly separable laboratories
  wins immediately, and the encoder's response is to reorganise rather than
  to mix. If adversaries are kept, they need the tissue label as input (so
  that only tissue-unexplained source information is penalised), gradient
  clipping, and monitoring of the discriminator accuracy during training.
- **Strong confounding is the real test.** At confounding 0.9 the
  supervised blocks lose source mixing (0.25-0.56) because tissue predicts
  the source; the CKA variants keep mixing at 0.75 but have no tissue
  structure left. No variant achieves both, which is the expected behaviour
  when the factors are not identifiable from the marginal distribution; the
  thesis should state the identifiability assumption explicitly rather than
  tune for a metric that cannot be satisfied.
- **DIP-VAE and MOSA's own adversary remain not worth pursuing** (section
  7); their numbers did not move.

### Recommendation for the start of the thesis

1. Use `supervised_nocond` as the working model and `midas` as the
   discriminator-based comparison. Both are cheap (50-60 s per fold on the
   public data) and deliver the two objectives of the proposal: batch-free
   reconstruction by block replacement and an interpretable, tissue- and
   EMT-structured latent space.
2. Replace the marginal CKA penalty with a conditional one (within-tissue
   HSIC or MMD, or a tissue-aware leakage discriminator) and evaluate it on
   `sim_c09`, where the trade-off is visible.
3. Report leakage as a curve and as an effect on downstream predictions,
   not as a single linear probe accuracy.
4. Move to the internal cohort with real organoids and tumours as soon as
   possible: the simulation fixes the *form* of the technical effect (shift,
   rescaling, programme shift, missing views), and the real effect may differ.
